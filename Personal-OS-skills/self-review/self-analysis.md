Your current bottleneck is not capability accumulation. It is **value capture**: turning broad cognitive strength into a clear market position, repeatable assets, and economic rent.

## Strengths

**1. Cross-domain system synthesis**
Finance → ads → recommendation → consulting → AI agents → org design. The value is not "knowing many things" — it is seeing structural analogies across domains and converting them into executable solutions.

**2. Business-to-technical translation**
You repeatedly convert vague business language into metrics, workflows, evaluation, and architecture. This is the core capability behind ads GMV/ROI work, recommendation strategy, enterprise AI scene selection, and agent evaluation loops.

**3. Causal reasoning depth**
ByteDance bilateral market spillover analysis, holdout experiments, dynamic load strategy — real causal inference under real constraints. Rare in ToB AI where most agent proposals have no closed-loop verification.

**4. Team building from zero**
8-person team, zero attrition, integrated algorithm/data warehouse/data science roles through multiple org changes. Real organizational leverage, not luck.

**5. First-principles commercial instinct**
Your reflections consistently return to: where is the terminal value? Who captures it? What is the real bottleneck? What is the deal? This is the raw material for commercial maturity.
