Last updated: 2026-05-30

## The Core Problem

个人收益 = 创造价值 × 个人价值捕获系数

创造价值能力还行，但捕获系数太低。原因：你是价值链里的一环，而不是"别人必须通过你"的节点。你一直在做 Beta，以为自己在积累 Alpha。

Beta: Prompt 工程、RAG 搭建、BI 看板、AI 方案 PPT — 门槛快速下降，难以形成个人壁垒。

Alpha: 判断哪些业务场景值得 Agent 化，判断 AI 项目是否有真实增量，把业务流程变成可评估的 Agentic System — 这件事现在还没有被充分定价。

## Market Mismatches (Your Alpha)

**Mismatch 1: Enterprises want AI but no one helps them decide what's worth doing.**
老板要 AI，业务要提效，IT 要系统，供应商说什么都能做——但没人帮企业回答：什么先做，什么不做，什么根本不该做。

**Mismatch 2: AI Agent vendors sell technology; clients need business outcomes.**
供应商讲多智能体、工具调用、工作流；客户关心多卖货、少浪费预算、提高复购。中间缺翻译层：用技术理解商业，用商业约束技术。

**Mismatch 3: Everyone chases tool Alpha; the real Alpha is in operating system redesign.**
AI 改变企业的终局不是"员工助手"，而是"经营操作系统"——AI 发现异常，AI 生成策略，人审批关键决策，系统自动跟踪执行。这个判断现在还是非共识。

These three mismatches point to one position: **AI 经营智能体架构师** — helping enterprises identify which operating workflows are worth agent-izing, and delivering evaluable, operable decision systems.

## Blind Spots

**1. Delivery depth mistaken for product depth.**
Dongshi projects (Apple, Sunshine, Taiyi) are high-quality custom delivery, not reusable products. Capabilities.md lists "Pharma AI" as domain expertise, but the self-assessment says "pharma industry understanding still shallow." You have delivery experience, not industry depth.

**2. Agent architecture is yours; agent engineering is not.**
You designed the dual-brain architecture, but OpenClaw/Hermes implementation details are not yet filled in. If you cannot independently implement core agent engineering modules, the architecture capability depends on the team.

**3. No unauthorized leverage.**
All value output requires organizational authorization: leading teams, meeting clients, making deliveries. No public writing, no open-source tools, no independently running product. If you leave the current platform, leverage drops to zero.

**4. Only managing the cash account.**
Work at Dongshi optimizes the cash account (KPI, delivery, revenue). The capability account (what portable skills did you build?) and position account (brand, standards, network effects) are almost unmanaged.

**5. Optionality without a wedge.**
Too many plausible identities: agent architect, data science leader, AI consultant, business strategist, technical generalist, personal OS builder. The market cannot price you correctly until you choose a sharper hill.

**6. Framework density hides execution gaps.**
You are good at naming systems. The failure mode is stopping at conceptual elegance. No framework is done until it has: a user, an example, a failure case, a decision it changes, a measurable before/after.

**7. Relationship leverage is underdeveloped.**
"Knowing people" ≠ relationship leverage. Leverage means: people bring you ambiguous opportunities before they are fully defined; people trust your judgment enough to change decisions; people introduce you because your category is clear.

## Recommended Position

```text
AI-native business decision systems
```

External sentence:

```text
I help growth, sales, and enterprise operating teams turn messy business workflows
into AI-native decision loops with data, evaluation, and execution feedback.
```

Why this works:

- Uses data science / causal / metric background
- Uses ByteDance ads and recommendation experience
- Uses current agent architecture and enterprise delivery
- Avoids being just another "AI agent builder"
- Stays close to business value and economic rent
- Can become consulting, productized service, public writing, or startup wedge

Do not lead with: tool names, generic agent frameworks, "I know many domains", personal OS abstraction, AI transformation as a broad category.
