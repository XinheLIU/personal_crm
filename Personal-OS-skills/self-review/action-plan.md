# Action Plan

Last updated: 2026-05-30

## Starting Principle: Effectuation over Analysis

目标模糊时，不要先问"我要成为什么"，先问"我手上已有的能力、场景、关系，能发起哪些低成本实验"。

可承受损失原则：凡是失败只损失少量时间和面子的动作，不要反复论证，直接做。

- 写一篇文章：2-4 小时
- 做一个诊断表：1 天
- 找 5 个人访谈：一周碎片时间
- 给熟人做一次免费诊断：2 小时

这些都是"下注周末"，不是"下注人生"。

## Leverage Principle

每做一次低杠杆工作（定制方案、一次性 PPT、重复分析），问：这能不能沉淀成一个可复用的框架、模板、工具、文章？

> 今天定制，明天模板，后天产品。

媒体杠杆是现阶段最适合启动的无授权杠杆。输出判断型内容，不是 AI 热点：企业 AI Agent 为什么常常只是 BI 换皮？哪些场景真的值得 Agent 化？AI 项目 ROI 应该怎么算？

## Phase 1: Package the Method (2 weeks)

Deliverables:

- 1-page positioning memo
- 1 diagnostic scorecard
- 1 anonymized case writeup (Apple, Sunshine, or O2O)
- 1 agent evaluation matrix
- 1 short essay

Success: Someone unfamiliar with your work can explain your value in one sentence.

## Phase 2: Run Effectuation Experiments (4 weeks)

Actions:

- 5 free/low-cost 60-minute AI workflow diagnostics (founders, enterprise operators, senior PMs)
- Publish or privately circulate 3 short memos:
  - Why most enterprise AI agent projects fail before technology starts
  - Scene → data → agent: a practical evaluation loop
  - The difference between AI demo, AI tool, and AI operating system
- Release 1 downloadable diagnostic template

Success: 3 external people reflect back the same value proposition. 1 asks for follow-up. 1 use case emerges you did not predict.

## Phase 3: Choose a Wedge (2 weeks)

See wedge comparison table in [strategic-planning.md](strategic-planning.md).

Recommended: **Growth / sales decision loops powered by AI agents and evaluated through data feedback.**

## Phase 4: Build Rent Assets (4 weeks)

Deliverables:

- 3 reusable templates
- 2 anonymized case notes
- 1 private distribution list or public writing channel
- 10 high-quality relationship conversations

Success: You own an asset that can travel across companies.

## Operating Rules

### Weekly Review

```text
What did I create this week that is reusable?
What did I learn from the market, not from thinking alone?
What relationship became stronger?
What did I ship that someone else used?
What did I do that only the company captures?
```

### Project Extraction Rule

After each major project:

```text
Extract method.
Extract proof.
Extract relationship.
Extract asset.
Extract positioning language.
```

If a project produces only internal delivery, it is incomplete for your personal strategy.

### Positioning Rule

Internal identity can stay broad. External sentence must stay narrow:

```text
I help [buyer] solve [urgent workflow problem] by [method] so that [measurable outcome].
```

Current draft:

```text
I help growth and enterprise operating teams turn messy business workflows
into AI-native decision loops with data, evaluation, and execution feedback.
```

### Commercial Ownership Questions

Ask before every project:

```text
Who pays?
Why now?
What budget does this replace or unlock?
Who gains power if this works?
Who loses power if this works?
What is the smallest proof that changes the deal?
What asset do I own after delivery?
```
